01 · Before they've heard of you

Brand and positioning

Why you, not the factory down the road.

Ask ten suppliers in your category what makes them different, and most will say the same three things: quality, price and service. A buyer can't choose between ten identical promises, so they choose on price.

BeforeWe are a leading manufacturer of high-quality LED lighting with competitive prices and good service.
AfterLED panels for UK electrical wholesalers: tested to UK standards, held in stock in Shenzhen, shipped in 7 days.
An example to show the idea, not a client. The second line tells a buyer who it's for, why it's safe and what to expect.

What we do

  • Talk to you, your sales team and, where we can, your best customers, to find out why buyers really choose you
  • Study the competitors your buyers compare you with, in their market and their language
  • Write your positioning: who you're for, what you're best at, and the proof that backs it up
  • Design a name, logo and visual identity if you need one, or sharpen the one you have
  • Give you a short brand guide: how you look, and how you sound in English

What you'll have

A clear answer to “Why should I buy from you?”, in words a Western buyer believes, used the same way on your website, LinkedIn, trade show stand and every sales email.

A logo on its own starts at €145. Brand + Launch, with a matching website, starts at €1,795. Full positioning work is quoted after your free review.

03 · When you reach out

LinkedIn and digital outreach

Conversations with buyers who fit.

Most export cold messages start with “Dear Sir/Madam, we are a leading manufacturer” and get deleted after the first line. Buyers see so many of them that a good supplier who writes the same way gets deleted too.

We don't buy followers or send thousands of automated messages. LinkedIn restricts accounts that do, and buyers can tell.

What we do

  • Rebuild your company page and your sales team's profiles, so a buyer sees real people they'd do business with
  • Build a list of buyers who fit: the right countries, company sizes and job titles
  • Write connection requests, messages and follow-ups that sound like a person, not a brochure
  • Write your posts: factory proof, customer stories and useful answers to buyers' questions
  • Set up email outreach to match, from a domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC in place so it reaches the inbox
  • Report every week: messages sent, replies and meetings booked

What you'll have

A steady stream of conversations with buyers you chose, and a team that knows how to keep them going.

04 · When you meet face to face

Trade show strategy and follow-up

Turn handshakes into orders.

A trade show is often the biggest marketing cost of the year. Then the team flies home with a stack of business cards, and the follow-up goes out weeks later, after the buyer has met dozens of other suppliers.

  1. Before the show

    Book the meetings

    Choose the shows your buyers attend, contact the buyers you want to meet, and prepare your stand message, brochures and samples.

  2. At the show

    Capture every lead

    A quick lead form for every conversation: who they are, what they need and the next step. A short review with your team at the end of each day.

  3. After the show

    Follow up while they remember you

    Personal follow-up within two working days, a planned sequence for the months after, and a report on which leads became quotes and orders.

What we do

  • Help you choose shows by where your buyers go, not by habit
  • Contact your target buyers on LinkedIn and by email to book meetings before you arrive
  • Write the stand message, brochures and sample kits for buyers in that market
  • Stand graphics, printed materials and branded giveaways made factory-direct, from 5 pieces on most items
  • Set up lead capture for the stand and review leads with your team each day
  • Write the follow-up emails and track every lead until it's won or closed

What you'll have

Every lead from the show captured, followed up and tracked to a quote or an order, so you know which shows are worth booking again.

05 · Every conversation after that

Cross-cultural sales training

A team overseas buyers want to deal with.

Your team's English may be good. But buyers in the US and Europe read a lot between the lines. A late reply sounds like a late shipment. A defensive answer to a complaint can lose a customer it took years to win.

Your team writesThe buyer hears
“No problem.”Did anyone actually check?
“We are the leading manufacturer.”Everyone says that.
“Price can be discussed.”So the first price wasn't real.
“Sorry for the inconvenience caused.”They're not going to fix it.
No reply for three daysIf they're this slow now, what happens when there's a problem?

What we do

  • Workshops for your sales team, built on your own emails and real buyer situations
  • How Western buyers think about risk, price and trust, and how that changes from market to market
  • Templates for the hard emails: quotes, delays, price rises and complaints
  • Practice for calls and video meetings, with feedback
  • Review of live emails for the first weeks, so the new habits stick

What you'll have

A sales team that writes and speaks in a way buyers trust, and a playbook that stays with your company when people leave.

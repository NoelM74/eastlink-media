Questions

Straight answers.

Is Eastlink Media the company I'll work with?

For sourcing, fulfilment and shipping, you work directly with the specialist company: China Factory Sourcing, China Fulfillment or Parcel From China. Each has its own team, prices and terms. Eastlink Media is the marketing company built by the same founder, Noel Murphy.

Can I use just one of the companies?

Yes. Each company works on its own. For example, you can source the goods yourself and use only China Fulfillment to store and ship them.

Do you ship to my country?

Our companies ship to more than 200 countries and regions. China Fulfillment quotes 6 to 10 days to the US, 5 to 8 days to the UK and 6 to 14 days across the EU.

How do I get a quote?

Use the form below and tell us what you're buying, roughly how many and where it's going, and we'll pass it to the right company. Or contact China Factory Sourcing, China Fulfillment or Parcel From China directly through their websites.

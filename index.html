The export trap

Most export growth stops at the first order.

If you sell from China, you'll recognise some of these.

  • Buyers ask for our best price, then go quiet.
  • On Alibaba we're one of dozens of factories, and the cheapest one wins.
  • The trade show cost a fortune. Three months later, nobody has replied.
  • Our website looks like a catalogue. Nobody sends an enquiry.
  • When a salesperson leaves, their customers leave too.
  • One big customer is most of our revenue. If they go, we're in trouble.

Your buyer isn't looking for the cheapest supplier. They're looking for the safest one.

A buyer in Chicago or Hamburg puts their own job on the line when they choose you. One late shipment or one bad batch, and it's their problem. So they pick the supplier who looks least likely to let them down, then stay with them for years.

That trust is built in small moments: the first line of your website, the first LinkedIn message, the follow-up after the show, the reply to a complaint. The Eastlink Borderfree System gets each of those moments right.

We keep enquiry data for up to 2 years from the date we receive it. We delete it sooner if you ask us to (see section 7), or once we no longer need it for the reason you sent it.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website doesn't set any cookies, and we don't use tracking or advertising tools.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It doesn't use cookies or fingerprinting, and it doesn't follow you across other websites.

If you change the currency on our prices, your browser's local storage remembers your choice for your next visit. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

The analytics script loads from Cloudflare. Everything else, including fonts, images and videos, loads from our own domain. Apart from Cloudflare, which hosts the website and runs the analytics (see section 4), no other company receives your IP address when you visit.

7. Your rights under the GDPR

You have these rights over the personal data we hold about you:

  • Portability: receive it in a portable format (Article 20)

To use any of these rights, email hello@eastlinkmedia.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

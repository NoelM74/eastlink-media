Fonts loaded from Google

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address. We don't control what Google does with it. Choosing "Essential Only" on our cookie banner doesn't stop these requests, because the site needs the fonts to display properly. You can block them with a browser extension if you prefer.

7. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have these rights over the personal data we hold about you:

  • Portability: receive it in a portable format (Article 20)

To use any of these rights, email hello@eastlinkmedia.com. We'll reply within 30 days.
